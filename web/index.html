Results

SplatDiff compares canonical voxel fields instead of raw Gaussians. Re-training a scene changes almost every Gaussian, yet SplatDiff reports almost no change; real edits are still found with high precision and recall.

How it works

Two 3DGS scenes of the same place contain different Gaussians even when nothing changed, because training is driven by image loss. SplatDiff compares what the Gaussians describe, not the Gaussians themselves.

01
Two scenes

Scene A at time t and scene B at t+1, each a set of anisotropic Gaussians.

02
Canonical field

Each Gaussian deposits its opacity-weighted volume into a voxel grid, mass-conserving and colour-independent.

03
Compare

Occupancy masks are compared with a 1-voxel dilation, so sub-voxel jitter is ignored.

04
Changes

Added and removed voxels are reduced to counts and drawn over the splats.
